# Global X Camera document library

> **Library scope:** this collection and its counts originated with the recovered September hardware and later dated candidate research. The [current specification](../spec.md) records the selected October prototype; the [prototype research record](../docs/prototype/prototype-parts-and-camera-research.md) records later camera and development-board evidence. An archived document or part entry does not establish that its part is still selected, and the historical counts below are not a live inventory.

Initially collected 2026-09-05 from the supplied BOM, EDIF netlist, and specification; candidate documents have been added during subsequent design work. The source PDFs are stored unchanged. This is a document collection and input inventory, not a completed electrical or footprint review.

**74 PDFs; 23 named BOM part entries; 146 EDIF instances; 81 nets.** Archive size: 186.4 MiB.

Start with the part coverage table below. Each document has an adjacent `.pdf.source.json` record containing its source and resolved URLs, retrieval time, page count, SHA-256 hash, and any variant or source caveat. [manifest.json](manifest.json) indexes the full library. [parts-inventory.json](parts-inventory.json) preserves the BOM fields and EDIF identifiers/connectivity.

## Coverage and remaining inputs

- Datasheets and directly relevant hardware, package, layout, programming, and errata documents are included where available.
- Generic resistors, capacitors, and inductors have no selected manufacturer part numbers. Their exact datasheets and operating curves cannot be chosen yet. General design references are labeled separately.
- CR2032 identifies a cell type, not a selected battery manufacturer. The Energizer sheet is a reference candidate, not a BOM decision.
- The Kyocera exact-MPN specification is a historical manufacturer-authored copy hosted on a TI forum. A current official Kyocera series sheet is also included; the two have different scope.
- D2's UMW/LCSC stock-code document uses SS34B as the SMB order code and SS34 as the marking. See [power/discrete coverage notes](discretes/coverage-notes.md) before purchasing or choosing a land pattern.
- The exact CAM-5G0-079CLR STEP archive was located but could not be downloaded in this session. Its verified manufacturer link and the available mechanical dimensions are recorded in [camera coverage notes](camera/README.md).
- See [schematic reconstruction inputs](SCHEMATIC-RECONSTRUCTION.md) for what can be recovered from the EDIF and what must be resolved.

Further notes: [processor document editions](processor/README.md), [memory/RTC variants](memory/coverage-notes.json), and [independent document QA](coverage-review.md).

## Refresh and verification

The tools in `../tools/` can rebuild the inventory and index. Downloads require Python with `pypdf`; the Windows download helper uses system curl with TLS certificate verification enabled. Re-running `index_datasheets.py` verifies every archived PDF against its recorded hash and page count. The unchanged BOM, netlist, and specification remain the source inputs.
