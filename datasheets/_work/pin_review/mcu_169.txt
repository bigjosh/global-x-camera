5.3.16 I/O current injection characteristics

                                             As a general rule, current injection to the I/O pins, due to external voltage below V    SS or
                                             above VDD (for standard, 3.3 V-capable I/O pins)  should be avoided during normal product
                                             operation. However, in order to give an indicati  on of the robustness of the microcontroller if
                                             abnormal injection accidenta lly happens, some susceptibilit y tests are performed on a
                                             sample basis during device characterization.
