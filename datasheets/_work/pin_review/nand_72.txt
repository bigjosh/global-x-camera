 8-2-1. WP# Signal

 WP# going Low can cause program and erase operations automatically reset.
 The enabling & disabling of the both operations are as below:


Figure 45-1. Enable Programming of WP# Signal                   WE#


                                                               IO[7:0]                 80h             10h


                                                                WP#                  tWW




Figure 45-2. Disable Programming of WP# Signal                  WE#


                                                               IO[7:0]                 80h             10h


                                                                                    tWW
                                                                WP#



Figure 45-3. Enable Erasing of WP# Signal                       WE#


                                                               IO[7:0]                 60h             D0h


                                                                WP#                 tWW




Figure 45-4. Disable Erasing of WP# Signal                      WE#


                                                              IO[7:0]                  60h             D0h


                                                                                    tWW
                                                                WP#
