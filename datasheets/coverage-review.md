# Independent documentation coverage review

Reviewed 2026-09-05. Scope: connectors, controls, the two CX3225SA crystal documents, and the two design-reference PDFs. This is a document identity and readability review, not electrical or footprint signoff.

- **Y1 has exact historical coverage, with a source limitation:** `sensors_timing/CX3225SA40000D0PTWCC_specification_TI_forum_copy.pdf` explicitly covers `CX3225SA40000D0PTWCC`. Its nine pages identify Kyocera drawing UKY1C-H1-18849-00[43], issued November 8, 2018 to Digi-Key. Page 3 specifies the exact 40 MHz, 8.0 pF variant, and page 5 gives its land pattern. The sidecar correctly identifies a Kyocera-authored, user-uploaded TI E2E copy; a current manufacturer-hosted controlled exact specification remains unavailable. The separate current CX3225SA series catalog should remain general coverage: its standard electrical table is not the selected variant specification.
- **Connector and control identity checks pass:** the Hirose drawing and specification identify `BM28B0.6-30DS/2-0.35V(51)` / CL0673-5021-0-51; GCT's USB4105 ordering grid covers `GF-A` with the default 0.95 mm shell stakes; Tag-Connect identifies `TC2030-IDC-NL`; Omron includes the selected side-actuated `B3U-3000P` without boss or ground terminal; ROHM explicitly identifies `SML-P11MTT86(R)`.
- **References are correctly qualified:** the Energizer CR2032 file is marked a candidate for an unselected battery manufacturer, and the Murata dynamic-model file is marked general guidance for unselected MLCCs. Neither claims to establish project component limits. The Tag-Connect sidecar correctly separates cable numbering from the project's custom SWD assignment.
- **Readability checks pass:** pypdf parsing and text extraction succeeded for all 13 reviewed PDFs. Byte lengths, SHA-256 hashes, and page counts match the sidecars. Hirose drawing pages 1 and 3, USB4105 drawing page 1, the CX3225SA catalog page, and exact crystal specification pages 3 and 5 were rendered with pypdfium2 at 2x and visually inspected. Package geometry, pad dimensions, pin labels, and drawing notes are readable without clipping. Hirose's geometry is predominantly graphical, so text-only extraction is inadequate for later footprint work.
- **Hirose metadata qualifications recorded:** the exact drawing sidecar now preserves the manufacturer's "HRS DRAWING FOR REFERENCE" qualification. The BM28 catalog and ETAD-H1016-00 product guidelines are now explicitly marked as series guidance in their sidecars, while retaining the applicable project MPN in the `parts` field.

No source PDFs were changed. Memory/RTC variant and coverage notes were moved into `memory/coverage-notes.json` for durable reference.
