## Current behavior and firmware contract

**Retention, approved October 14:** successfully committed photos and the metadata needed to recover them must survive **ten years at room temperature (approximately 25°C reference), fully unpowered, with no refresh needed**. No periodic wake, battery/RTC supply, USB connection or maintenance write may be required for photo retention. Implement the selected device's ECC and qualify both data and metadata through **1,000 complete fill/download/explicit-erase cycles**, approved October 14; the minimum 200-photo capacity implies 200,000 photo saves. Account for actual block and metadata wear. Optional USB maintenance and marginal-read handling remain separate implementation choices. See [retention review](decisions/storage-retention-review.md) and [spec.md §6.7](../spec.md#67-photo-retention--approved-october-14-2026).

**Capture and capacity:** STM32N6 remains active; Himax is abandoned. The prototype uses CAM-6G3-073CLR / VD56G3 with 73° diagonal field of view and uncompressed packed RAW10 at full native resolution. Retain at least 200 photos between downloads. Each packed image payload is 1,916,420 bytes before metadata; actual capacity, NAND part/interface, allocation and reserve policy remain to be qualified. The former 644 × 604 VD55G0 image describes the recovered circuit only.

**Timing and input:** with USB disconnected, a shutter press starts the main supply; it must last until early firmware establishes GPIO HOLD. From fully powered off, **start of exposure must occur within ≤50 ms of the shutter-button press**. Qualify this deterministic design budget; no runtime timing-error code is selected. Exposure duration, frame readout, save and shutdown are measured separately afterward. Ignore additional presses during capture/save, finish the current operation unless a fault requires abort, and queue no exposure. A new capture requires an actual power cycle of the main capture electronics after shutdown and a fresh press after ready; a busy press held through completion must not become a deferred shot.

**Storage:** preserve existing committed photos and refuse new captures when no usable slot remains; indicate full using the status LED. Full payload readback plus checksum comparison is configurable and enabled by default during bring-up/initial testing; it may be disabled later. In either mode, generate integrity metadata/checksums, wait for program completion, check status/failures, perform required ECC/bad-block/recovery handling, and read back and verify the final durable commit record before successful-save shutdown. ECC status does not verify an unread payload. The criterion for later disabling full readback remains unsettled; firmware and the final storage format remain unimplemented.

**Power and shutdown:** four series CR2032 cells through LTC3621 are selected. The room-temperature 100 nA OFF target applies to the complete main path, including regulator and external pack-connected leakage; retained RTC cell-tap backup is additional and must be measured/budgeted separately. After safe save, prepare peripherals and shutdown configuration, then enter genuine N6 Standby while an ordinary HOLD GPIO remains driven until entry. Standby makes that GPIO high impedance; Stop retains its configuration. Retain external pulldowns, select a pin outside the documented exceptions and prevent held-button wake retriggering. In the existing divider/HOLD concept, a held button keeps the buck enabled until release. External PWR_ON/AUX sequencing and any optional RUN-to-ground shutdown delay remain unimplemented: AUX must discharge below 0.6 V before main supplies leave their valid range. See [power.md §6](../power.md#6-camera-integration-requirements).

**Shutdown definition and F-01, approved October 13:** shutdown is the lowest attainable power state, with an actual power cycle of the main capture electronics required before another image; RTC backup may remain powered. A held button waits for release to permit main power-off, with no automatic wake/retake. At any phase of a cycle, including post-save shutdown, detected low battery requires **one red LED flash**, then abort to shutdown. This abnormal abort must not wait for successful image commit. Preserve existing committed photos, including the current image if already valid and durably committed; never report an incomplete or unverified image as saved. Interrupted writes follow the accepted USED/DONE policy; physical encoding, conservative recovery and device qualification remain open. Indication and controlled shutdown require enough remaining power, not a guarantee after rail collapse. Battery sensing, thresholds and warning/hold-up energy remain to be implemented and qualified. The recovered [BOM](../bom.txt) has green/yellow-green **LED1, ROHM SML-P11MTT86R**; a red-capable indication must be selected and qualified, with no CAD/BOM change made here.

**F-02 abrupt power loss, approved October 13:** the current uncommitted image may be incomplete, but preserve previously saved photos and an already validly committed current image. **Keep any bytes written to NAND.** Mark blocks **USED at save start** and **DONE at successful completion**; USED without DONE identifies possibly corrupt/incomplete data. On restart recover valid, complete, durably committed photos, without silently erasing/discarding incomplete raw bytes or automatically taking a replacement exposure. Excluding incomplete data from successful-photo recovery does not permit physical deletion. Marker layout/encoding, NAND geometry, reclamation and recovery remain unimplemented; qualify torn markers, programming limits, shared-block effects and preservation of older photos. USED/DONE does not replace mandatory status/integrity checks or final commit-record readback verification. No hardware guarantee, final-write opportunity or LED indication after abrupt collapse is assumed. See [spec.md §6.5](../spec.md#65-f-02-abrupt-power-loss--approved-october-13-2026).

**Failure map, approved October 13:** low battery at any phase = **1 red flash**; camera initialization = **2**; frame capture = **3**; storage unresponsive = **4**; new-photo write, enabled-readback or commit failure = **5**; damaged allocation metadata/index = **6**. These faults enter the defined shutdown state after indication where power permits, require a main-capture power cycle before another image, and do not trigger an automatic retake. No flash duration, repeating pattern or retry count is selected. If time or orientation is unavailable/invalid, record an explicit **BAD sentinel**, still save the image, and omit that unavailable EXIF field when presenting the photo over USB. Report existing-photo read failures to the host during USB reads; report explicit-erase failures/interruptions through USB. Reading a photo does not authorize erase. Boot failure/firmware hang has no selected recovery requirement; this does not assert technical impossibility. See [spec.md §6.6](../spec.md#66-remaining-failure-responses--approved-october-13-2026).

**USB protocol review, October 14:** the user is considering standard photo-object transfer and deletion without a filesystem. [PTP is proposed](decisions/usb-photo-access-review.md) as a replacement for drive emulation, pending approval and host testing; per-photo deletion is not yet selected. The existing USB baseline follows.

**USB:** USB powers download/explicit-erase mode independently of the batteries and the shutter has no operation. Emulate a host-visible USB mass-storage drive presenting photos as files. Choose the easiest practical emulated filesystem and photo representation during prototype firmware work; host write/delete semantics are not selected. Connecting USB or downloading does not erase photos; reclaim saved-photo space through an explicit USB erase action after download. Insertion/removal during operation is allowed; seamless power continuity and concurrent host-write behavior are not thereby selected. USB detection, source isolation and unintended battery-converter enable remain hardware integration work, including the Nucleo test arrangement.

**Deferred directions:** VD5943/custom optics, spring-driven mechanical frame advance and FD-03 series-capacitor pulse start remain future work. FD-03 could permit shutdown despite a held button; it is not the optional RUN-to-ground delay and does not replace the current divider/HOLD baseline. No capacitor values or SPST-reset-bleed/SPDT arrangement are selected.

The recovered specification records a 40 MHz HSE, execution from internal SRAM after early boot loading, and primary/redundant FSBL locations of `0x00000` / `0x40000`. Reconcile these inherited implementation inputs with boot ROM behavior and the existing firmware; they are not independently validated boot settings.

The recovered VD55G0 camera connection uses one CSI-2 data lane plus differential clock, a 1.8 V configuration bus, an MCU-generated master clock and shutdown/analog-enable controls. Its historical sequence keeps shutdown asserted, establishes core and I/O rails, delays analog enable by approximately 1 ms, establishes MCLK and releases shutdown. Do not carry these values into VD56G3 unchanged: verify the selected module's supplies, connector, supported lane/mode, clocks, register sequence and exposure-start timing.

## Work sequence while firmware is integrated

1. Use the completed KiCad reconstruction and its evidence as the historical starting point. Apply the selected camera/power integration and confirmed electrical corrections, recording intentional connectivity changes separately.
2. Review the revised supplies/latch, default states, voltage domains, pin functions, boot wiring and footprints. Use the integration table to resolve interfaces that can change copper.
3. Establish placement groups, decoupling and power-loop placement, BGA escape feasibility, camera/USB routing corridors, and mechanical reserves using a conventional four-layer process as the working target.
4. Reconcile the firmware's existing pin/clock configuration, boot/startup sequence, sensor mode, and storage integrity design when those artifacts arrive. A concise pin map and startup timing outline are useful even before the complete firmware tree is integrated.
5. Freeze affected pin assignments and high-speed routing constraints after their checks; obtain actual fabricator stackup and manufacturing rules before final routing geometry and release verification.

Firmware integration runs alongside hardware work. It is required to validate the shared interface and end-to-end behavior; it does not block circuit corrections, footprint validation or placement planning. The 1.6 mm/four-layer PCB is provisional, and the ≤5 mm finished-product thickness is a design target that may be exceeded by the functional prototype.
