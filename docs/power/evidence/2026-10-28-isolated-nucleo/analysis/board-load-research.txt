Offline non-Ethernet board-load review — 2026-10-28

Scope: official MB1940-N657X0Q-C02 schematic/BOM plus manufacturer datasheets.
Camera and SD are physically disconnected in the new campaign (root report).
These are circuit/specification estimates, not measured savings. Root owns hardware.
Ethernet is being investigated independently; no Ethernet savings are asserted here.

Highest-value order
1. Measure matched CPU busy/sleep and clock-divider controls with identical LEDs,
   external clocks and rails. The bounded probe's busy case is an LFSR/SRAM load,
   not the normal SD polling loop; it estimates workload sensitivity, not automatic
   capture-cycle savings. Use native GPIO edges as wall time across sleep.
2. Separately resolve Ethernet state and measure its controlled contribution.
3. Measure main VDDIO and auxiliary1.8V rail currents at their measurement links.
   Their LDO losses scale with load and can matter more than regulator bias current.
   Neither rail is disposable merely because a camera or SD card is absent.
4. Keep unused LEDs and USB-C control circuitry off. Avoid small-component rework
   until branch measurements identify worthwhile loads.

Documented remaining loads and limits

U33 LD39020ADTPU33R: 5V→mainVDDIO3.3V LDO, always enabled. No-load bias20µA
typical (~0.10mW at5V). Its conversion heat is approximately1.7V×rail current:
e.g.40mA would mean68mW. This is a formula/example, not a measured40mA load.
An efficient replacement rail could recover much of this loss; changing the whole
board input to3.3V is not an equivalent drop-in test because other rails need5V.

U23 LD39020ADTPU18R: 5V→always-on1.8V LDO. Same20µA no-load typical bias;
conversion heat approximately3.2V×load current. Required AON rail, not a spare.

U26 LD56100DPU18R: auxiliary3.3V→1.8V LDO,100µA typical no-load bias
(~0.33mW at its3.3V input), plus1.5V×rail current. LCON grounded selects the
0.5A current-limit setting, not a low-quiescent-current mode. Changing LCON
does not constitute a power-saving mode. EN follows PWR_ON; do not disable
the rail without auditing every MCU/sensor/NOR/analog consumer and pin state.

U17 TPS62088YFP: core buck already uses a low-IQ architecture:4µA typical
with EN high, no load, not switching. That20µW figure is NOT total operating
converter loss. Actual conversion loss depends on core load. This is the
non-A part; the forced-PWM A-family8mA no-load maximum does not apply.

U34 ST1S31: auxiliary3.3V buck. Datasheet gives630µA typical no-load bias
with duty cycle0/VFB1.2V (~3.15mW at5V), not a guarantee of total in-circuit
light-load loss. Light-load pulse skipping reduces switching loss. Its rail
also feeds required circuitry; don't remove U34 simply to save its bias.
U14 ST1S31 is ALREADY disabled: R22 EN link and R10 output link are not
fitted, and floating EN has an internal pulldown. Do not count another630µA
saving. U25 VIN→5V regulator is not supplied with VIN/CN9 paths disconnected.

U7/U8/U9: six TSZ122 ADC-buffer channels,28µA typical/channel at1.8V:
168µA or0.302mW at the IC supply. Their feed via a1.8V LDO costs roughly
0.554mW at the3.3V upstream rail, before buck losses. No enable input;
removal loses analog-header paths. A very low-priority physical modification.

U22 TCPP03-M20: datasheet OFF/reset with EN0 specifies no DC draw from
VCC_VCONN/EN; normal mode permits2.7mA VCC draw. Verify PA7/EN and mode
before assigning any normal-mode load. With CN8 absent, VBUS indicators and
USB-power path are not part of the powered input. Do not credit2.7mA as a
real saving when the controller was already off or hibernating.

ST-LINK voltage-sense dividers: R65/R70=10k+10k on mainVDDIO means165µA;
R73/R74=5.1k+10k on target5V means331µA. Since mainVDDIO is LDO supplied,
their combined contribution at5V is approximately0.496mA/2.48mW. This is
an ideal-resistor calculation; divider taps may have additional leakage.
Removing them sacrifices debugger voltage sensing and is not the first lever.
D2 and R23 are already removed. Do not count debugger main power or LD1 twice.

User LEDs LD5/6/7 and Arduino LD3 can be turned off by firmware. Their
remaining current must be measured or calculated using each actual resistor
and LED voltage. The isolated probe deliberately holds all three status LEDs
off in both windows. No microphones appear in the official C02 fitted BOM.

U31 NOR identity caveat: official C02 BOM specifies MX25UM51245GXDI00,
512Mb. The software library's MX66UW name does not prove installed silicon.
For the BOM part, official March2026 datasheet p95 gives standby20µA typical
and180µA maximum; deep power-down3µA typical/50µA maximum at1.8V. Typical
silicon saving is only30.6µW once CS is idle. Don't subtract maxima to claim
a guaranteed saving. Confirm actual JEDEC ID before any command experiment;
all executing code/data must be in RAM and recovery/boot wake behavior proven.

Startup capacitance
C14 is fitted220µF directly on target5V: Murata GRM31CR60J227ME11,
6.3V X5R MLCC,1206,±20%. Nominal constant-C0→5V charge is C×V=1.10mC;
stored energy is0.5×C×V²=2.75mJ. Effective capacitance falls with DC bias;
these are reference calculations, not exact expected charge/energy. For a
voltage-dependent capacitance use the integrals of C(V)dV and V×C(V)dV.
The nominal values are the same order as the previously
measured first5ms charge~0.94mC and unqualified raw energy~3.7mJ. It makes
bulk-cap charging plausible, not proven: capacitor tolerance, actual DUT rail
voltage, other capacitances/loads and JS transient behavior remain unresolved.
Do not remove bulk capacitance without validating rail droop and startup.
Soft-start/current-limited input is a more useful first controlled experiment.
