# Compact native-sample RAW-cycle energy

Generated by the unchanged v66c exporter. Every finite sample in the selected view contributes to signed sums and extrema. Bins split exactly at current onset, plateau start and confirmation boundaries; no fractional-bin approximation is used.

Run `python firmware/capture-raw/energy_compacts.py replay --folder PATH` without the original raw arrays. This reproduces the four energy intervals and primary durations/medians. It cannot rerun raw onset/plateau detection or recover within-bin sample order. SBF attribution and image readback remain separate evidence.

The legacy 9.5 A validity threshold and recorded startup spikes are unchanged. No analog calibration, saturation correction, or isolated-DUT energy is inferred.
