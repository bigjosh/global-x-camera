Cold normal-cycle PHY integration comparison

Scope: offline only. Root owns all hardware, firmware installation, cold cycling,
card reads, and state qualification. Analysis files stay in this directory.

Run three original-firmware baseline cycles and three PHY-power-down candidate
cycles with the same source, camera/card, wires, scene/exposure, marker protocol,
and cold-off interval. Preserve each run, including any failures. Prefer an
additional baseline reversal if a material unrelated drift is observed; do not
automatically expand testing after a clean controlled result.

Read native current/voltage/power and packed IN0/IN1 by sample ID. Require saved
file hashes, continuous chunks, native 2 MSPS, unique complete marker IDs1..10
and semantic precedence. Keep all startup chatter/unclassified edges. Exclude
the entire last packed GPIO chunk because valid padding length is unresolved.

Use first >=20 mA onset with preceding finite 1 ms near zero. Exclude first5ms
from qualified energy comparisons, as in the prior direct5V archive. Plateau
boundary is the first whole1ms bin after marker10 followed by ten bin means
inside6..12mA. Also report marker10 endpoint separately. Retain whole/first5ms
raw integrals explicitly unqualified. Report low plateau over100ms.

Compute actual power means and energy from native power.f32, not mean V times
mean I. Split at actual marker ordering, preserving exposure/SD-init overlap.
Report SD initialization, first-slot submission to transport return, commit,
deinit and shutdown separately. Three repetitions quantify the observed range;
they do not establish a population distribution or prove every time reduction
was caused by PHY power-down. Present slot-transport mean power alongside its
duration so card-latency variation stays visible.

Firmware identity, exact installation readback, PHY action proof, saved-image
readback and Standby flags are separate evidence supplied by root. The analyzer
does not derive these qualifications from current shape or markers.

Invocation: python -B analyze_cycles.py input-manifest.json
Manifest entries: run, group baseline/candidate, trace path, optional evidence.
Relative trace paths resolve against tmp/bringup/ethernet-normal-20261028.
Dependencies are two inert offline modules in power-ablation-20261028; their
hashes are saved in comparison.json. No imported module executes hardware.
