Interpretation: the reproducible before/gap/after reversal establishes that
scheduling affects the approximately193mA peak. It supports overlapping demand,
but this test also gives the electrically powered card an extra200ms before
initialization. It does not isolate overlap from card startup aging. A later
STOP->immediate-SD experiment could distinguish these while avoiding deliberate
waiting; none was performed here.
