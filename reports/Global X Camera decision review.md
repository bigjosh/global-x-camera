## Current position

The recovered product concept is a thin, credit-card-size monochrome global-shutter camera: a shutter press starts the electronics, one full-resolution image receives time and orientation metadata, storage completes, and main power turns off. USB provides downloads independently of the batteries. The documented priorities are acquisition latency, total shot energy, thickness, area and procurability, in that order. These are the coherent existing baseline; this audit does not treat every sentence in the specification as a separately approved user decision. ([Product and priorities](<../spec.md>))

The Flux recovery and KiCad migration are real accomplishments. The reconstruction accounts for 148 components, 737 terminals and 81 nets; it preserves the recovered connectivity and records its additions. A readable edition also exists. **On October 12, a fresh export of the current schematic matched the September reviewed connectivity baseline.** The current parts remain STM32N657X0H3Q, W25Q128JVPIQ boot NOR, MX30LF2G18AC-TI parallel NAND, TPS62132/TPS62825 regulators and AO3401A battery switches. The readable edition rearranges presentation; it does not implement the proposed fixes. ([Reconstruction](<../hardware/reconstruction/README.md>), [readable edition](<../hardware/kicad-readable/README.md>), [fresh evidence](<../research_notes/Global X Camera decision review/current-cad-evidence.json>))

The September register contains **47 findings: 5 blockers, 22 high, 17 medium and 3 low**. It distinguishes confirmed conflicts, conditional requirements and matters requiring measurement; these are not 47 independent product decisions. No completed routed PCB, manufacturing release, integrated camera firmware or measured complete capture/save/shutdown cycle was found. The zero-error ERC result demonstrates consistency of the reconstructed schematic model, not a working or manufacturable camera. ([Findings register](<../docs/review/findings.md>), [review limits](<../docs/DESIGN-REVIEW.md>))

Ten-year room-temperature retention with no power or refresh is now approved. The 1,000 complete reuse-cycle lifetime and PTP with explicit per-photo deletion are also approved. ECC/CRC-32C, best-effort export and whole-slot retirement on predictive media errors are now approved. The `_RECOVERED` filename suffix plus EXIF UserComment is approved for damaged exports. Handling retired data is settled: every host image-data request freshly reads the whole original NAND image into RAM, checks it and exports that result with warnings. Repeated reads may differ. No image relocation, refresh or background payload scans; only explicit host deletion removes photos. ### 2 Shutter response target — settled for the prototype

**Settled:** From fully powered off, **exposure must start within 50 ms of the shutter button press**. The interval includes power-up, processor boot and camera initialization. Exposure duration, frame transfer, storage commit and shutdown may finish after this deadline and must be measured separately. This approval defines the requirement; it does not establish measured compliance. Verify the start of the exposure that produces the saved image, rather than a preliminary/discarded frame. Identify the module revision and instrument actual exposure start during cold-start tests, then record light/exposure conditions, timing variation and complete save/shutdown latency. ### 3 Thickness and OFF-current budget — settled for the prototype

**Settled:** Four CR2032s in series through LTC3621 for the prototype; ≤5 mm is the finished-product target and a thicker functional prototype is allowed. The user has specified a **0.1 µA (100 nA) room-temperature OFF target for the main power path**, superseding the earlier <1 µA target. **RTC backup is retained and budgeted separately as additional cell drain**, by explicit user approval. **Proposed approach:** screen regulators and reject parts with excessive leakage; this is not yet a qualified screening process. **Implementation scope:** The 100 nA main-path allowance includes the regulator plus input-capacitor, switch/PCB and other pack-side leakage; it is not a regulator-only allowance. The recovered design powers RTC backup directly from one cell, and the power study identifies input-capacitor, switch/PCB and other battery-side leakage outside the regulator. Its RV-3028-C8 is configured for Level Switching Mode; the manufacturer's table gives 140 nA typical / 210 nA maximum backup current at 3 V and 25°C with VDD below 2 V. That is a separate cell load and cannot be removed by screening the buck. [RTC operating parameters, p97](https://www.microcrystal.com/fileadmin/Media/Products/RTC/App.Manual/RV-3028-C8_App-Manual.pdf#page=97). Test the assembled power stage at the intended pack voltage after settling, with appropriate fixture resolution, and record the RTC cell-tap current separately. Do not treat the regulator's typical shutdown number as a whole-camera result or assume screening yield. ([Battery and mechanics review](<../docs/DESIGN-REVIEW.md>), [OFF budget](<../power.md>))

### 4 Storage and data integrity

**Settled:** Retain at least **200 full-resolution, uncompressed packed-RAW10 photos between downloads**. Each VD56G3 image is 1,916,420 bytes before metadata. A 4 Gbit (512 MiB) device holds 280 tightly packed payloads before overhead; planning for 2 MiB per image and a 20% overall reserve gives 204 slots. These calculations do not qualify usable capacity or freeze an exact NAND part. The earlier approximately 800-image estimate applies to VD55G0 only. [Sensor format](https://www.st.com/resource/en/data_brief/cam-56g3.pdf). **Readback decision, October 13:** Full-image readback and checksum comparison are configurable, enabled during bring-up and initial testing, and may be disabled later. Both modes still require program-completion/status checks, integrity metadata/checksum generation, a verified final commit record and interrupted-write recovery. ECC status must be handled whenever data is read; no-read mode does not establish that the payload has been read back successfully. Benchmark complete save time and energy in both modes. The switch does not waive retention or power-interruption testing. **Storage-full decision, October 13:** Preserve existing photos and reject new captures when no usable image slot remains. Indicate full with the status LED; reclaim space through an explicit USB erase action after download. Connecting USB or downloading alone does not erase photos. The usable-capacity minimum remains 200 photos; the exact full-indication pattern and erase interface are implementation details. **Failure-policy decisions, October 13:** Mark allocation USED before payload saving and DONE on successful completion. Retain interrupted bytes as occupied/possibly corrupt; preserve prior photos and do not automatically erase/reuse suspect data. Report existing-photo read and explicit-erase errors over USB. Store BAD time/orientation values and omit the corresponding unavailable EXIF fields during USB file generation. The physical format and conservative recovery remain to implement. **Retention decision, October 14:** Retain successfully committed photos and recovery metadata for ten years at room temperature (approximately 25°C reference), fully unpowered, with no refresh needed. No periodic waking, battery power or USB maintenance may be required. Qualify this through the accepted 1,000 complete reuse cycles; no compliance claim is established yet. **Reuse decision, October 14:** Support 1,000 complete fill/download/explicit-erase cycles, equivalent to at least 200,000 photo saves at 200 photos per fill. Retention still applies to images saved after rated usage. Exact memory/interface and retention qualification remain to complete. **Recommendation:** Qualify the proposed 1.8 V quad-NAND path, retaining a qualified alternative, before declaring a winner. Use the final image format and a common complete-save test, including metadata, ECC/status checks, verification and shutdown. Implement the accepted power-interruption, retention, 1,000-cycle reuse and PTP/per-photo-deletion requirements. **Read policy approved and refined October 14:** each host image-data request freshly reads the whole original NAND image into RAM, checks ECC/CRC-32C, then exports that request's buffer with filename/EXIF warnings. Fully corrected/CRC-valid pixels remain verified; damaged bytes are exported best-effort. Predictive media errors permanently retire the whole slot from future writes without deleting its current image or preventing later host reads. Later reads may differ. No image relocation, refresh, background payload scans or substitution of an earlier request's RAM buffer; the USB copy-out proposal is superseded. Only explicit host deletion removes photos; retirement persists afterward. Catalog metadata reads and capture-time verification remain separate. Current-read PTP filename timing, RAM capacity and predictive thresholds need qualification. The object policy is settled: store pixels and photo attributes together once, without separate copies. Storage-control redundancy is implementation-dependent. D-32 approves best-effort retrieval and explicit deletion of PARTIAL objects with EXIF and/or PTP warnings. Slot allocation/wear is the next discussion. The tradeoff is important: Micron’s modeled write-only advantage nearly disappears against Winbond when full readback is included, and longer MCU-on time can outweigh lower NAND energy. microSD adds a switched 3.3 V rail and socket, with cold-start and durable-commit behavior needing qualification. ### 5 Power and shutter control

**Settled:** LTC3621 supplied by four series CR2032 cells is the prototype front-end regulator direction. **Accepted control direction, October 13:** Use the button-fed battery divider to assert RUN, with a diode from GPIO to RUN providing HOLD after early firmware takeover. The user accepts a minimum press duration until HOLD is established and accepts low-power waiting after the save if the button remains held; main power then switches off on release. This follows the existing RUN/HOLD study. Forced power-off while the button remains held is not required for the prototype. **Future direction FD-03, October 13:** retain a series-capacitor start pulse as a possible defense against held-button battery drain; reset/rearming, reliable boot-to-HOLD timing and leakage/transients need qualification. This is separate from the optional RUN-to-ground shutdown-delay capacitor below. The held state includes divider current, the enabled regulator's quiescent consumption, converted MCU/peripheral loads and separate RTC backup; it is not the 100 nA button-released OFF state. **User clarification:** after a successful safe save, firmware prepares peripherals and the shutdown state, then enters the lowest-power mode; the intended HOLD release occurs as part of that transition. RM0486 Rev 4 §13.7.7 states that ordinary GPIOs become high impedance without pull-up/down in Standby, subject to its listed pin exceptions; Stop retains I/O configuration. Select a suitable ordinary HOLD pin and retain the external pulldown so Standby itself can release the diode-fed HOLD. An optional capacitor from RUN to ground may delay buck disable after HOLD releases, but no value is selected. Coordinate the resulting delay with auxiliary-rail discharge before main supplies fall, and check startup latency, component tolerances/leakage and rapid button release/repress. The capacitor holds RUN enabled; the battery and regulator continue supplying the MCU during that interval. Exact timing, held-button Standby current and the integrated shutdown circuit remain unqualified. [RM0486 §13.7.7, p383](https://www.st.com/resource/en/reference_manual/rm0486-stm32n647657xx-armbased-32bit-mcus-stmicroelectronics.pdf#page=383). **USB decision, updated October 14:** USB supplies a PTP camera interface with explicit per-photo deletion, replacing drive emulation. Shutter presses do nothing. Download alone never deletes photos; the exported image format remains to select. Keep the battery regulator disabled while USB powers the camera; source detection, reverse-current isolation and a hardware RUN inhibit still need implementation. Ignoring the button in firmware cannot suppress its direct divider-to-RUN path, and the HOLD diode does not establish reverse-power isolation. On the Nucleo, use the target MCU's CN8 USB port for PTP; CN10 is the ST-LINK port. **Busy-press decision, October 13:** ignore additional shutter presses while capturing or saving, finish the current photo, queue no delayed exposure and require a fresh press after ready. **Remaining implementation:** Apply the accepted failure map; complete downstream rails, regulator sizing/passives, shutdown timing and USB/battery source integration. **Recommendation:** Evaluate 1.8 V digital power within the selected architecture and qualify parts against the battery/load envelope. Qualify short taps, held/repeated presses, USB insertion/removal and shutdown during saving against the accepted policies. Boot failure/hang has no additional prototype recovery response; a stuck HOLD can keep the supply enabled. The proposed RUN/HOLD circuit needs the button held until firmware takes over and cannot turn off against a held button; 1.8 V changes its hold margin and RTC backup relationship. These need an integrated solution, not a regulator substitution. Close with a reviewed power-state sequence and voltage-domain schematic, followed by measured cold start, brownout, source transitions and controlled shutdown; boot/hang remains an accepted limitation. ([RUN/HOLD behavior](<../power.md>), [integration requirements](<../power.md>), [1.8 V consequences](<../docs/power/1v8-memory-assessment.md>))

## Recommended discussion order

Completed prototype decisions: **CAM-6G3-073CLR at 73°; four series CR2032s → LTC3621; button press to start of exposure ≤50 ms from fully powered off; ≤5 mm finished-product thickness target with a thicker functional prototype permitted; 0.1 µA room-temperature OFF target for the main power path, with RTC backup additional and separately budgeted; at least 200 full-resolution, uncompressed packed-RAW10 photos between downloads; configurable full-image readback, enabled for bring-up and initial testing and disableable later; preserve existing photos and stop accepting new captures when storage is full; button-fed RUN divider and diode/GPIO HOLD, accepting a minimum press until HOLD and automatic GPIO release on Standby entry, with held-button Standby permitted; USB-powered PTP interface with explicit per-photo deletion, shutter inactive, exported image format still open; ignore additional shutter presses while capturing or saving, with no queued exposure and a fresh press required after ready**. The full failure-response map, ten-year room-temperature retention with no power or refresh, and 1,000 complete storage reuse cycles are also settled. ECC/CRC-32C, read warnings, predictive-error slot retirement and fresh whole-image NAND-to-RAM reads on each host request are also approved. No relocation or background image reads. Remaining discussion order: **single stored photo object and PARTIAL recovery selected; journal provisional → slot allocation/wear (current) → remaining storage/power qualification → manufacturing package → optional features and firmware-stack decisions**. Discuss one item at a time. Recover existing firmware and prepare justified corrections in parallel; missing Claude history is not a gate.

**Failure review complete:** the user supplied responses for every case. Red codes 1–6 cover low battery, camera initialization, frame capture, unresponsive storage, failed new-photo save and damaged index/allocation respectively, followed by shutdown. Preserve prior photos and retain interrupted USED/not-DONE allocations as possibly corrupt. Report stored-photo read and explicit-erase errors over USB; record BAD time/orientation values and omit unavailable EXIF fields on USB export. USB connection changes are allowed; boot/hang has no additional prototype response. The 50 ms limit is a deterministic design/qualification requirement, with no runtime late-shot error selected. **Retention is also settled:** ten years at room temperature, fully unpowered, with no refresh required. **Reuse is also settled:** 1,000 complete fill/download/explicit-erase cycles. **USB interface is also settled:** PTP with explicit per-photo deletion, superseding drive emulation. **Read policy is also settled:** device-appropriate ECC plus original-image CRC-32C; return verified photos normally and recover as much damaged-image data as possible with integrity diagnostics. Permanently retire the whole image slot from future writes when read errors indicate future media unreliability, while keeping existing data readable. Routine corrections with a matching CRC are not damaged images. Successful transfer of a recovered object does not assert the original image passed verification; real transfer failures still report errors. **Damaged-image warnings are also settled:** `_RECOVERED` before the filename extension plus EXIF UserComment with available integrity/error details. **Retained-data handling is settled:** every host image-data request freshly reads the entire original NAND image into RAM, checks it, and sends that buffer with current-read warnings. Repeated reads may succeed or fail independently. Only explicit host deletion removes the image. Predictive media errors retire the whole slot without blocking reads or causing deletion. There is no image relocation, refresh or background payload scan; the copy-out proposal is rejected. RAM sizing, warning-name timing and retirement classification remain to qualify. **Object policy is settled:** pixels plus name/time/orientation and other photo attributes are one stored object, with no duplicate copy of any object contents. Redundancy for journals, checkpoints and other storage-control records depends on their implementation; this supersedes the proposal for duplicate photo metadata. Existing ECC, original-image CRC-32C and commit verification remain. **PARTIAL recovery is settled, D-32:** return whatever safely attributable image data can be read, including from incomplete objects, with read errors/incompleteness reported through EXIF and/or PTP. Retain _RECOVERED naming and explicit-only deletion. Missing final CRC means unverified; missing EXIF or optional attributes must not block recovery. Retrieval never certifies a successful original save. **Current discussion:** slot allocation and wear distribution. **Slot-state persistence is provisionally selected:** keep a RAM table backed by redundant append-only NAND journals and checkpoints. The fixed-slot working model has FREE/PARTIAL/FULL occupancy and GOOD/BAD health. Exact reserve size, object/control record layout, recovery and storage-control redundancy remain to qualify. **Future directions retained:** move the table off NAND, and/or store allocation/completion/health state within each slot, potentially using supported vendor bad-block markers. A first-page FF read alone cannot prove safe FREE after interrupted erase. A final completion record must be validated against the current allocation. Preserve factory markers, permanent retirement, retained-photo read access and immutable physical mappings. See D-30 and FD-04/FD-05 in the repository. Physical marker encoding, recovery, LED timing and safe source transitions are implementation work. The repository's failure response review is the authoritative full checklist.
