```text

   │                  gate
   └──── pull-up ───────┤
                       ├── button ────────── GND
                       └── drain [N-MOS] source ── GND

                                      ├── CPU HOLD
                                      └── pull-down ── GND
```

- Press button → power turns on.

**A low-side NMOS can switch power, but this direct-GPIO arrangement has a ground-reference problem.** Your divider limits voltage relative to battery negative; the CPU measures voltage relative to its own ground.

```text
BAT+ ── regulators + CPU ── CAMERA_GND ── [N-MOS] ── BAT−
                                │                     │
                          CPU’s reference       gate’s reference
```

While the MOSFET is **on**, those two grounds are nearly equal, so the CPU can hold it on.
