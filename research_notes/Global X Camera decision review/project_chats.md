## 2. Open questions and conflicts that the consolidated report should preserve

### Takeaway

Separate **decisions to make** from **known corrections to apply** and **qualification evidence to collect**. Most repeated discussions reopen part choices, while the September design review already identifies concrete electrical faults that should not be presented as optional architecture opinions.
